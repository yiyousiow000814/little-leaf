# Chef food contact and active flame

Six persistent food pieces occupy the pan. A working portion moves after the spatula reaches it, then follows a push, collection, short lift, release and settle. There is no second stationary copy or disappearing garnish. Ingredients occlude the blade on the food plane, while the raised blade supports its carried portion.

The 2.6-second presentation cycle uses the existing cooking clock. The final 0.45 seconds settles the tool, body and food within the original recipe duration, including upgraded recipes ending mid-lift. The active flame, rigid spatula shaft, articulated fixed-length arm, planted feet and small body shift follow the same state.
