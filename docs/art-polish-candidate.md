# Head and tabletop-flower candidate

This is a visual-review candidate, not an accepted art direction or release.
Baseline: PR #91 at `104bcf116745f6496ca4dfb4853bc6b9e7f240a7`, locally restored as `f432d125f0eb2ba20ac7590c1c66c16798d084da` with the same tree.

## Observed problem

The current native register and sink captures show very circular, flat pale heads. Ear roots and the chin merge into one silhouette, especially from behind. Both eyes have identical size despite a three-quarter facial plane. The tabletop decoration is one stem and one oval leaf; there is no flower. These simplified marks feel less finished than the shaded ceramic equipment and illustrated HUD.

## Small, coherent style standard

- Soft toy-like anatomy: distinguish crown, cheek and chin with a continuous rounded outline. Keep species identifiable by ears and muzzle, rather than adding detail indiscriminately.
- Use one restrained shadow family, warm cream highlights and muted green-brown facial ink. Far-eye foreshortening should be subtle; expressions must survive ordinary play zoom.
- Attach ears behind the crown; keep mouth/nose on the muzzle plane. Back views need a readable skull/chin transition rather than a featureless circle.
- Small props should have a complete silhouette and material cue before fine detail: ceramic rim, shaded side, branching stems, leaves and recognizable blooms.
- Normal play zoom is the primary review. Medium zoom reveals construction problems but is not the acceptance scale.

## Boundaries and evidence

Uniform actor scale, torso and limb geometry, planted feet, furniture masks, cabinet/stove geometry, dining anchors, simulation and saves are unchanged. The head remains inside existing contact and atlas envelopes. The flowers retain the existing vase anchor and are independent of plate/cup ownership.

The dedicated native capture runs the same generated register scene in all four orientations at normal and medium zoom for baseline and candidate. Exact source hashes, camera receipts and original PNG hashes are retained. Review pending actual candidate pixels; passing geometry tests does not establish visual acceptance.
