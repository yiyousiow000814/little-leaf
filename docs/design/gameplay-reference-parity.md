# Gameplay Reference & Parity

Draft gameplay foundation, 2026-10-11. Source audit: Little Leaf main **50dd65f5de1d476019497966206f88cf38cd9c96**, covering the canonical v0.1.10-v0.3.2 roadmap and inspected model, service, staff, queue, dishwashing and economy consumers. This adapts completed read-only evidence, not a new audit or implementation. Product scope and completion remain in the [canonical roadmap](../roadmap.md). No gameplay, economy, save, activation or release policy is changed. Final mechanics, visual and audio approvals remain user-owned.

The user's latest direction makes practical preservation of Restaurant City's mechanisms the design goal, with original art, animals, audio and world behavior added. Several roadmap restrictions predate that clarification. They need explicit reconciliation rather than silently becoming implementation defaults.

## Highest-impact findings

1. **Ingredient allocation, recipe growth and functional unlocks need a coherent loop.** The roadmap proposes permanent ingredient mastery, automatic equipment/ingredient unlocks, a small main-dish menu and three recipe ranks. These are explicit simplifications, not completed parity. Historical mastery could unlock functional equipment: the September 2010 Coffee Bar combined stove/drink functions; February 2011 Pizzeria tied mastery to equipment and a minigame. Retaining only XP and plating differences would lose those strategic consequences.
2. **No trading and no arcade income are product exclusions.** Solo progression can remain viable without making social ingredient exchange impossible. A free patience-only arcade removes an independently documented economic/interaction mechanism. These are user decisions, not evidence gaps.
3. **Choose a staff-management era before calling energy missing.** Early feeding/rest and later selectable shifts are alternatives. Official May 2010 replaced feeding/resting with opening shifts; beds became nonstacking speed buffs. Little Leaf's duty/payroll control matches neither system completely, but restoring stamina alongside shifts is not automatically required.
4. **Functional furniture and saved layouts deserve more than cosmetic parity.** Original examples include patience/popularity buffs, multi-customer seating and three, later four, saved layouts. Little Leaf's two-blueprint proposal and cosmetic table variants are narrower. One table/one customer cannot be generalized across all original eras.

## User decision proposal and minimal next plan

Present four choices together before roadmap edits: (1) early staff feeding/rest, later opening shifts, or explicitly adapted duty/payroll; (2) recipe-specific ingredient investment/category selection/mastery unlocks versus the current reduced loop; (3) solo-completable progress with optional exchange/help versus an explicit no-trading adaptation; (4) functional furniture, arcade play/income and saved-layout breadth to retain. Recommend preserving these decision loops, while adapting punitive social locks, premium monetization and raw balancing only by explicit agreement. This is a recommendation, not approval to implement.

Then update the single canonical roadmap only under parent/user authority. Define one small recipe→ingredient→mastery→functional-equipment journey first, with frozen-order rewards and historical-save migration boundaries. Compare alternative layouts/staff allocation through the separately owned Living World journeys (`docs/design/living-world.md`); use [testing guidance](../testing/README.md) for generic Game Feel/QA. Measured responsiveness, strategic choices and subjective enjoyment remain separate evidence. Garden/offline/social systems follow their own dependency and security decisions. No broad traffic, route or rendering rewrite follows from this audit.

Static pixels support visible labels, quantities, amounts and composition, not animation timing, refill effects, snapping, universal footprint rules or operational function. Combining the independently reviewed pixels with dated official sources can narrow context; it does not make a single example a universal rule.

Private source files, exact image identifiers/hashes, Library IDs and transfer paths remain in the historical review's private evidence. Public documentation records inspected scope and unresolved details without exposing those identifiers. References are research inputs and never Little Leaf assets; do not import original pixels. The final public historical reference summary, when published by its owner, should be linked here without duplicating the pixel review.

Open details are early energy/refill formulas; universal menu-slot/rank progression and upgrade costs; popularity/arrival/expansion formulas; garden maintenance/yields; universal furniture rotation/footprints/editor behavior.
