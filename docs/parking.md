# Fixed customer parking (0.1.10 candidate)

Decorate → Decor includes one fixed four-bay parking upgrade. Its provisional price is 2,000 in-game coins, configured by `CafeParking.PRICE`. It does not grant building floor, move furniture, expand land, or generate parking revenue. The exterior lot, flush driveway, green buffer, and short walking link keep their approved positions. Before purchase, the lot surface, bay markings, crossing, and walking link remain hidden, leaving the original lawn. Selling restores that lawn. Only a purchased lot is drawn, with real customer cars when visits exist.

A successful purchase stores its original paid cost. Selling within the same uninterrupted Decorate session refunds that cost; Done or a successful load ends this receipt and subsequent sales return half the paid cost. Autosaving alone does not end the receipt. Any reserved or occupied car trip blocks selling, including arrivals, queueing, dining, walking back, and driving away. Failed/repeated actions do not change the wallet.

Parking uses the existing four-second demand clock and at most two attempts per batch. Alternate eligible guest IDs may arrive by car, with ordinary walking fallback when parking or pending-request capacity is unavailable. Each car reserves one of four bays and one outside waiting slot before arrival. Inbound reservations and the existing outside queue share its six-request bound. Older prospective requests cannot be overtaken. Cars use a single serialized throat/aisle maneuver; cars are never spawned on an occupied road origin. Customer simulation continues when the camera faces elsewhere; decorative street traffic and bus passengers remain separate.

Drivers appear after parking, follow the buffer link to the public pavement, then enter through existing admission or join its FIFO queue with the same identity and exact handoff position. The normal outside 90-second waiting timeout and seated-service patience remain unchanged. Closing, timeout, and unfinished meals return the driver to the car. A completed indoor exit transfers its physical position to the parking return without releasing unfinished table cleanup or replaying payment. The bay is released only after the vehicle reaches its exit endpoint.

The optional `little_leaf.parking.v1` save object stores ownership, paid basis, trips, routes and progress; it never stores the temporary refund receipt. Missing parking data migrates to unowned/empty without a charge or source rewrite. Present malformed data fails before live state changes. Parking-only exterior geometry is validated separately, without widening staff/service bounds. Older binaries that ignore this optional field cannot preserve parking on a subsequent save; do not downgrade an owned-parking save to an older build.

Headless focused tests cover state transitions, same-ID handoffs, admission/queue ordering, cancellation/reopening, resale, migration, exact numeric roundtrips and malformed-save rejection. UI and draw-command tests are separate from real rendered acceptance. Final native/mobile screenshots, browser persistence/resume and actual-device performance remain release checks, not claims made by these tests.
