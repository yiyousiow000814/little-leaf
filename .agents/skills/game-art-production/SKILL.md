# Game art production

Read the [Art Bible](../../../docs/art-audio/ART_BIBLE.md), the affected
inventory/reference rows and the [roadmap](../../../docs/roadmap.md#current-state).
Confirm the asset owner, authorized phase and current pauses there. Preserve each
reference's recorded approval scope; a draft or tool proof is not Golden approval.

- Pin source/tree and inspect actual original pixels at normal game scale.
  Classify the work as an intentional art upgrade or behavior-preserving
  optimization; use the Bible's different approval/parity gates.
- For code-only optimization with unchanged art and authoring/export toolchain,
  preserve existing editable sources and calibration, then prove Pixel Parity;
  skip new GUI-asset proof and grid-authoring work below.
- For new/changed assets or authoring/export toolchains, follow the Bible's
  [tool choice and editable production](../../../docs/art-audio/ART_BIBLE.md#tool-choice-and-editable-production)
  rules. Prove the selected editable save/reopen/edit/export route and disclose
  its actual limits. Generated code is not graphical authoring proof. This skill
  alone does not authorize accounts, upgrades, installations or a paused lane.
- Calibrate a visible unit grid, logical footprint, pivot and contacts; reuse
  valid source-bound calibration where unchanged. Keep named editable parts and
  reproducible export settings. Review design before integration and preserve
  the asset's existing behind/actor/front relationships. Do not change cameras,
  routes or collision to fit art.
- Follow the renderer-owned depth contract through the Bible's
  [maintained contracts](../../../docs/art-audio/ART_BIBLE.md#authority-and-existing-contracts)
  for anchors, parts, actual order and cache ownership. Do not invent a sorting
  architecture, magic offsets or broad renderer rewrite.
- Retain source/export identities and follow existing
  [licenses](../../../docs/art-audio/README.md), import/atlas and
  [performance rules](../../../docs/testing/rendering-performance.md).
  Identify retained ownership, invalidation and per-frame cost before the
  smallest owner-relative integration.
- Use synthetic profiles and the minimum affected checks. Capture actual
  same-scene normal-scale Before/After and representative performance, then
  apply [game-art-review](../game-art-review/SKILL.md). Keep source, exported
  bytes and inspected captures bound together; disclose missing evidence.

Return editable source, export/integration identities, evidence and unresolved
choices. Follow the Bible's applicable pilot and refinement gates before new
standards are confirmed. Automated checks cannot approve visuals or update a
Golden Reference. This skill grants no additional publication, merge or
deployment authority.
